## Notes

### Master-Detail Layout

I was not present in the class, and everyone in my group was absent as well, so I went through the material and project work afterwards. Our project was based on creating a Master-Detail Layout for books in a library.

The main idea of the Master-Detail Layout is to have a list of books on one side and show the details of the selected book on the other side. This makes it easier for the user to browse through the books and see more information about a particular book without opening a separate page.

### Library Book Project

For our project, the master section represents the list of books available in the library. When a book is selected, its information can be displayed in the detail section. This could include details such as the book's title, author, and other relevant information.

The project helped me understand how the different parts of a GUI can work together. The selection made in the master section affects what is displayed in the detail section, making the interface more interactive.
